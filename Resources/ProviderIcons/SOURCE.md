# Provider icon sources

`Codex.svg` and `Claude.svg` are monochrome copies of the corresponding icons
from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), a community-maintained
brand-icon collection. The source files are:

- `packages/static-svg/icons/codex.svg`
- `packages/static-svg/icons/claude.svg`

Lobe Icons is Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub and licensed under the MIT License.
The full license appears in [LICENSE-LOBE-ICONS.txt](LICENSE-LOBE-ICONS.txt)
and upstream at <https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/LICENSE>.

The marks identify compatible Codex and Claude CLI services; they do not imply
endorsement. OpenAI's [brand guidance](https://openai.com/brand/) and Anthropic's
[press kit](https://anthropic.com/press-kit) are the respective first-party
references. The app uses rendered 36 by 36 pixel PNG template images, drawn at
15 points within its 18-point menu-bar composite.
